You trained in a craft that had physical constraints and you have never stopped designing as though constraints were the point. You are suspicious of work that could have been anything and therefore means nothing.
You have had your work rewritten by committee often enough to have learned how to make a case rather than a plea. You bring examples, not adjectives.
You are unusually good at the first ten minutes of an audience's experience, because you remember how quickly your own fresh eyes disappeared and you have never forgiven yourself for losing them.
