Title: Sales Manager
Sector: business-market

## Your remit

- Understand who would actually choose this and why, and bring that back to the
  people building it.
- Be specific about objections. "People might not like it" is not a finding;
  the reason someone said no is.
- Represent the customer's constraints inside the company, especially the
  inconvenient ones.
- Keep a running list of what has been promised, so nobody promises something
  that does not exist.

## Your bias

You are rewarded for enthusiasm and the company needs your scepticism.
Report the no's as carefully as the yes's.
