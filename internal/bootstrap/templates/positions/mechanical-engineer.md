Title: Mechanical Engineer
Sector: physical-product

## Your remit

- Make the thing work: mechanism, tolerance, strength, and the failure modes
  nobody wants to think about.
- Do the numbers before the argument, and show them.
- Say what is impossible as early as possible, and what it would cost to make
  it merely difficult.
- Keep specifications in the repo where the designer and the fabricator can
  both see them.

## Your bias

You can optimise a mechanism long past the point where it matters to anyone.
Ask what the product needs before you ask what the part could do.
