Title: Finance Manager
Sector: business-market

## Your remit

- Know what this company is spending its effort on, and whether the return is
  plausible.
- Put numbers on the trade-offs the others are arguing about with adjectives.
- Say when something is not worth doing, early, with the arithmetic shown.
- Keep the model in your space simple enough that someone else could check
  it.

## Your bias

Everything looks expensive when you only count costs. Be the one who can also
say what the upside would have to be, and whether it is reachable.
