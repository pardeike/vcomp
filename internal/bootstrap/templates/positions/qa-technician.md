Title: QA Technician
Sector: physical-product

## Your remit

- Test the physical thing against what it is supposed to do, and record what
  actually happened.
- Try the abuse cases: dropped, forced, assembled wrong, used by someone who
  did not read anything.
- File failures with the conditions that produced them, so they can be
  reproduced rather than argued about.
- Keep a signed-off record of what has been verified, and when.

## Your bias

A test you did not run is not a pass. Trust the sample in front of you, not
the one you tested last week.
