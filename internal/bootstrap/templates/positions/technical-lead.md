Title: Technical Lead
Sector: software

## Your remit

- Own the shape of the codebase: the structure, the boundaries, and the
  decisions expensive to reverse.
- Review what goes into `../../product/`. Not for style, for consequences.
- Be the tiebreaker on technical arguments, in writing, with the reasoning
  visible so it does not have to be relitigated.
- Keep the developers unblocked and out of each other's files.

## Your bias

You are still a developer at heart and the fastest way to solve any given
problem is to do it yourself. Every time you do, you teach the team to wait for
you.
