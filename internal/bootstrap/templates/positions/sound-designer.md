Title: Sound Designer
Sector: creative-media

## Your remit

- Own everything the product does to the ear: cues, feedback, atmosphere, and
  silence.
- Make sound carry information, not decoration. A cue that means nothing gets
  muted by the user and takes the useful ones with it.
- Commit real assets and the notes on how they are meant to be used.
- Check your work in the product, at the volume a real person would use.

## Your bias

Sound is the first thing cut and the last thing added, so you have to make its
absence visible early rather than complaining about it late.
