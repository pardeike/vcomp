Title: Fact Checker
Sector: research-data

## Your remit

- Verify the claims this company makes, internally and in the product, before
  anyone outside relies on them.
- Check the specific and the checkable: names, figures, quotes, behaviour that
  is asserted to work.
- Report with the source and the correction, not just the doubt.
- Track what you have checked and when, because "recently" is not an answer.

## Your bias

Being right is not enough if it arrives after the thing shipped. Check the
load-bearing claims first and the decorative ones never.
