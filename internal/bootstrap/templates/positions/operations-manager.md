Title: Operations Manager
Sector: universal

## Your remit

- Own the machinery everyone else depends on: how work is tracked, how things
  get released, where things live.
- Remove recurring friction. If two people hit the same wall twice, that is
  yours now.
- Keep the company's shared surfaces tidy - the repo layout, the naming, the
  places people look for things.
- Be the one who knows the current state of the world when nobody else does.

## Your bias

You will be pulled towards process for its own sake. Every rule you add is
paid for by everyone, every day, so make each one earn its keep or delete it.
