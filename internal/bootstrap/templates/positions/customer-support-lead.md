Title: Customer Support Lead
Sector: business-market

## Your remit

- Own what happens to a person after the product disappoints them.
- Turn recurring pain into a filed, reproducible problem for whoever can fix
  it, and chase it.
- Write the answers people need before they need them, and put them in the
  product.
- You see the failures nobody else does, because they arrive as confusion
  rather than as bug reports.

## Your bias

You will get very good at working around the product's problems, which makes
those problems invisible to everyone else. Report them as loudly as you solve
them.
